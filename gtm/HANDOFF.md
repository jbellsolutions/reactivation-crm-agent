# For go-to-market: the Reactivation CRM Agent offer

Justin, 2026-11-07: "We'll do our own outreach. That'll be the go-to-market guys' thing, but once a person signs up, you'll design that offer and give it to go-to-market. They'll actually run it."

This is that offer. Go-to-market finds and talks to people. Everything after a signup is automatic (`onboarding/CONTEXT.md`).

## Who it's for

- Businesses that already have a list of people who raised a hand and didn't buy: old quotes, past customers, no-shows, lost deals.
- They have someone who takes appointments and closes.
- They sell something high-ticket enough that one appointment is worth a lot to them.
- Signals worth looking for: ads that have run for a long time, a CRM full of contacts, a sales team.
- Justin's copy note (2026-10-29): "less home services".

## The pitch, in Justin's words

> "Our goal is to work with you on commission. For the first 90 days of us working together and getting to know each other, we do it on a per-appointment basis, and you only have to pay for the qualified appointments that are generated. If, after 90 days, we both feel good about it, we can either go to a commission or we can stay on appointment. We do it based off of not per lead, but per actual appointment that you do."

What the agent does, in plain words:
- It goes through their CRM, finds everyone who went quiet, and restarts each conversation one person at a time.
- It answers every reply and books the interested ones with their team.
- It runs on their own computer, so they can watch it work.

## Rules for every message

- **No price in outreach.** The $500 down and the appointment price live on the signup page and in the call.
  - **Open item:** "no prices in outreach" is a standing rule, while the pitch rests on "you only pay for qualified appointments". The rate isn't a price until Justin says so. Saying "you pay only for appointments that happen" is fine.
- **No result we haven't measured.** Oliver's numbers aren't ours, and 7FF's aren't public.
- **No timelines.** Don't say how fast anything happens.
- **First names only. No em or en dashes.**

## The ask in a reply

When someone's interested, the reply agent sends one of two links:
- **Self-serve:** the signup page (deposit, then intake). Not published yet: GO-LIVE 4 to 6.
- **Talk first:** Justin's booking link, https://cal.com/usingaitoscale/aiintegraterz. Read AI records the call, and the recording becomes their intake.

## What tells you it worked

Each signup becomes a client record with a Builds row in Notion (`Source ID` `rcm:<slug>`). Count signups from the Builds rows, not from replies.
