# Agreement template: Reactivation CRM Agent

**Draft. Not legal advice. Whether a lawyer reviews it is Justin's call (GO-LIVE.md).** It's accepted at checkout, through Stripe's terms of service consent, so it has to be live at a public URL before the deposit link is.

Between **{{business}}** ("Client") and **Revenue Partners** ("Provider"), effective when the Client pays the deposit.

## 1. What Provider does

Installs and runs the Reactivation CRM Agent on the Client's own Orgo computer:
- it restarts conversations with the Client's past leads and customers;
- it answers replies and books qualified appointments with the Client's team;
- and, if chosen at signup, Lead Radar.

Provider keeps support access to that computer for troubleshooting while this agreement runs.

## 2. What Client provides

- The Orgo computer, the model key, the CRM and its sending, on the Client's own accounts and at the Client's cost.
- Sign-in to those systems, done by the Client.
- Approval of the offer and the first messages before anything goes out.
- A mark on each held appointment inside the dispute window.

## 3. Fees, first 90 days

- **Deposit:** $500, held as credit.
- **Price per Qualified Appointment:** ${{price}}, drawn from the credit.
- When the credit can't cover the next Qualified Appointment, Provider requests a top-up and may charge the card on file for it. Campaigns pause while a top-up is unpaid.
- Nothing is charged for leads, bookings or no-shows.

## 4. Qualified Appointment

As defined in the Qualified Appointment schedule (`QUALIFIED-APPOINTMENT.md`), with a dispute window of 72 hours, a duplicate window of 90 days and an existing-customer window of 60 days. Each charge comes with its evidence on the weekly statement.

## 5. After 90 days

The parties choose either commission at a rate they agree in writing, or continuing per Qualified Appointment. Until they choose, the per-appointment terms continue. Unused credit: {{unused_credit_rule}}.

## 6. Consent and sending

- The Client warrants that every contact it provides gave the consent each channel requires. Written consent is required for marketing texts.
- Contacts without text consent on record get email only.
- The Client indemnifies Provider against claims arising from contacts the Client provided without that consent.
- Opt-outs are honoured at once.

## 7. Data

- The Client's data stays in the Client's accounts and computer.
- Provider uses it only to run this service.
- The Client can remove Provider's access at any time. Doing so ends this agreement.

## 8. Ending

- Either party may end this agreement on written notice.
- Qualified Appointments held before the end are payable.
- The Client keeps the computer, its agents and its memory. Provider's updates stop.

## 9. Limits

- Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages.
- Provider's total liability is limited to the fees paid in the 12 months before a claim.
