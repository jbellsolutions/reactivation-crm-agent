# The readout call: what the recording has to capture

For clients who want to talk first. Justin runs it; Read AI records it. The intake form is still the record of their answers (the Factory keeps emails and phone numbers private, so they can't come from the call). The call is where the questions get answered well.

1. **The business:** its name, what it sells, and the area it serves.
2. **Who books:** the rep's first name and their booking link. Their calendar must have open slots.
3. **What a good appointment is:** two to four criteria, in their words (for example "owns the home", "roof older than 10 years").
4. **The lists:** old quotes, past customers, no-shows, lost deals. Roughly how many of each, and where they live.
5. **Consent:** for each list, did these people agree in writing to get texts? Yes, no, or not sure. Anything not a clear yes gets email only.
6. **The CRM** they use, and who can sign in to it.
7. **Orgo:** do they have an account? If not, they buy a computer during install, on their own card.
8. **The model:** their own OpenRouter key, or Orgo's AI credit.
9. **Lead Radar:** yes or no, and if yes, where their buyers ask for help (groups, platforms).
10. **The terms:** $500 down, the price per qualified appointment, what counts, the dispute window, day 90. They accept at checkout, not on the call.
