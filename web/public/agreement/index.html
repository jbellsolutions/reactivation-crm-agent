Lead generation agreement

We will always do our best to meet your goals, but it's best to have a few things written down, so we both know what's what, who does what, and what happens if something goes wrong. You won't find complicated legal terms or long passages of unreadable text here. We have no desire to trick you into signing something you might later regret.

In short. We restart conversations with the people already in your CRM who went quiet, answer every reply, and book the interested ones with you.

What you pay for. Only qualified appointments: the person showed up, they're interested and want to do business, and they're in your area. Not leads, and not no-shows. Payment is always made in advance: your $500 deposit is credit toward them, at the price set for your business.

For the first 90 days it's per qualified appointment. After that, if we both feel good about it, we move to commission or stay per appointment.

Your leads. We do not share your leads with any other service provider in your territory, and we never put you in a bidding war with your competition. Everything runs on your own accounts. Texts go only to people who agreed to get them. You approve the first messages before anything goes out.

The whole agreement, small print included: read it here.
