How it works

  1. Put your $500 down and answer a short form about your business and your lists.
  2. You get one line to paste into Claude Code. It sets up your own Orgo computer and the agents on it, with Better than GrokBot as your window onto them.
  3. You sign in to your CRM on that computer's screen. The agents never see your password.
  4. You approve the offer and the first messages once. Then the agents restart conversations, answer every reply and book with your team.
  5. Each week you get a statement: every appointment, whether it counted, and why.

Your accounts, your data

The agents run on your own computer and your own accounts, and you can watch them work. You pay your computer, AI and texting providers directly. Texts go only to people who agreed to get them in writing.
