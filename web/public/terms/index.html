The agreement

You accept this when you pay the deposit. Between you (the Client) and Revenue Partners (the Provider), effective when the deposit is paid. Your price per qualified appointment is set for your business and written into your own copy before your deposit opens.

1. What we do

We install and run the Reactivation CRM Agent on your own Orgo computer. It restarts conversations with your past leads and customers, answers replies and books qualified appointments with your team. If you chose it, it also runs Lead Radar. We keep support access to that computer for troubleshooting while this agreement runs.

2. What you provide

The Orgo computer, the model key, the CRM and its sending, on your own accounts and at your cost. You sign in to them yourself. You approve the offer and the first messages before anything goes out, and you mark each held appointment within the dispute window.

3. Fees, first 90 days

The deposit is $500, held as credit. Each qualified appointment draws your price from the credit. When the credit can't cover the next one, we ask for a top-up and may charge the card on file; campaigns pause while a top-up is unpaid. Nothing is charged for leads, bookings or no-shows.

4. Qualified appointment

Booked through the agent's link by someone it worked, held, and the person is interested and fits the criteria you gave us. It doesn't count if it's a no-show, if you mark it not qualified with a reason within 72 hours of it being held, if the same person already counted within 90 days, or if they bought from you within the 60 days before. A held appointment you don't mark within 72 hours counts. Every charge comes with its evidence on your weekly statement.

5. After 90 days

We choose together: commission at a rate we agree in writing, or staying per qualified appointment. Until we choose, per appointment continues.

6. Consent and sending

You warrant that every contact you give us gave the consent each channel needs, including written consent for marketing texts. Contacts without text consent on record get email only. You indemnify us against claims from contacts you provided without that consent. Opt-outs are honoured at once.

7. Data

Your data stays in your accounts and on your computer. We use it only to run this service. You can remove our access at any time, which ends this agreement.

8. Ending

Either of us may end this agreement by written notice. Qualified appointments held before the end are payable. You keep the computer, its agents and its memory; our updates stop.

9. Limits

Neither of us is liable for indirect or consequential damages. Our total liability is limited to the fees you paid in the 12 months before a claim.
