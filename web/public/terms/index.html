The lead generation agreement

We will always do our best to meet your goals, but it's best to have a few things written down, so we both know what's what, who does what, and what happens if something goes wrong. You won't find complicated legal terms or long passages of unreadable text here. We have no desire to trick you into signing something you might later regret.

In short

You're hiring us (Revenue Partners) to restart conversations with the people already in your CRM who went quiet, answer every reply, and book the interested ones with you. You sign this on the agreement page. It starts when your first month is paid.

1. What we do

We set up the reactivation agents on a cloud computer in your own name, and they work your past leads and customers. If you chose it, we also find new people asking for what you sell. We keep support access to that computer for troubleshooting while this agreement runs.

2. What you do

You have the power to enter this agreement for your business. You give us what we need, when we need it: access to your CRM and the lists in it. You sign in to your own accounts yourself. You approve the offer and the first messages before anything goes out. The computer and the AI it uses run on your own accounts, at your cost, and we show you what they cost before anything is spent.

3. Payments

Payment is always made in advance. You pay a flat $500 for your first month, then a flat $1,000 a month. There is no fee per lead, no fee per appointment, and no commission. If a monthly payment doesn't go through, campaigns pause until it's paid.

4. A qualified appointment

It's what the agents work toward, and what your weekly report counts: the person showed up, they're interested and want to do business, and they're inside your service area. Leads and no-shows aren't counted. It doesn't change what you pay.

5. Your leads

We do not share your leads with any other service provider in your territory, and we never put you in a bidding war with your competition. When we work, we work directly for you.

6. Consent and sending

You confirm that every contact you give us gave the consent each channel needs, including written consent for marketing texts. Contacts without text consent on record get email only. You cover us against claims from contacts you gave us without that consent. Opt-outs are honoured at once.

7. Your data

Your data stays in your accounts and on your computer. We use it only to run this service, and we keep confidential anything you give us. You can remove our access at any time, which ends this agreement.

8. Ending

Either of us may end this agreement by written notice. You aren't charged for any month after it ends. You keep the computer, its agents and its memory; our updates stop.

9. Limits

Neither of us is liable for indirect or consequential damages. Our total liability is limited to the fees you paid in the 12 months before a claim.

But where's all the horrible small print?

You can't transfer this agreement to anyone else without our permission. If one part of it becomes invalid or unenforceable, the rest stays in place. The language is simple, but the intentions are serious: this is a legal document.

The line you sign: “I would like to get started with the program and agree to pay $500 for the first month, then $1,000 a month.”
